Frameling

A frame-by-frame animation sketchbook for iPhone.

Draw a picture. Tap plus. Draw the next one. Press play and your drawing moves. Export it as an MP4 or a GIF.

No ads. Ever.

Frameling shows no advertising of any kind and contains no advertising SDK. It works fully offline. Nothing you draw leaves your device.

One-time purchase

Frameling is free to use. Everything you draw can be exported at 720p with no watermark, for free, forever. A single one-time purchase unlocks 1080p and 4K export plus PNG sequences. There is no subscription.
