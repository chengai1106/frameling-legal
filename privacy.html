Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

The short version

Frameling has no accounts, no sign-in, and no advertising or cross-app tracking. Your animations are stored only on your device — we cannot see them, and they are never uploaded. We collect only minimal, anonymous product-interaction events to understand how features perform and improve the app.

What stays on your device

AnimationsEvery project, frame, layer and brush stroke you draw.
ThumbnailsSmall preview images the app generates for the project list.
PreferencesFrame rate, canvas size, onion skin opacity, selected tool and colour.

This data lives in the app's own storage. Deleting Frameling deletes it. It is included in your iCloud device backup if you have one, and Apple's privacy policy governs that backup — we have no access to it.

Exporting and sharing

When you export an animation, the file is written inside the app's own storage and handed to the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is entirely your choice. If you choose to save it to Photos, iOS asks your permission and Frameling writes only the file you just exported. Frameling never reads your photo library.

Purchases

If you buy the one-time unlock, the purchase is handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, which we use to check whether a purchase is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier it generates, a per-vendor device identifier supplied by iOS (Apple's identifierForVendor, which is not the advertising identifier and cannot be used to track you across other companies' apps), the purchase receipt Apple issues, your device model and OS version, and your country. It does not receive your name, email address, Apple ID, or anything about what you drew.

Apple processes the payment itself. We never see your payment details.

Anonymous product analytics

Frameling sends lightweight, anonymous product-interaction and purchase-funnel events (such as opening the app, starting a blank animation or starter template, adding frames, completing an export, viewing the PRO unlock screen, or completing/restoring the one-time PRO unlock) over HTTPS to PostHog (EU Cloud, hosted in the European Union). Events use a random anonymous identifier stored locally inside the app (erased if you delete Frameling), disable personal profile processing, and include basic app version and device type (iPhone or iPad). We never collect your name, email address, Apple ID, payment details, advertising identifier, or any part of what you draw.

This is what the App Store privacy label reports as Identifiers (for app functionality), Purchases (for app functionality and analytics), and Usage Data → Product Interaction (for analytics), none of which are linked to your identity or used for tracking (NSPrivacyTracking = false).

What we do not do

No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking SDKs, no email list, and no selling or sharing of data. Apart from the purchase check and anonymous product-interaction events described above, Frameling makes no network requests. Your drawings never leave your device unless you choose to export and share them. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Children

Frameling is a general-audience drawing tool. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personally identifiable data about you, there is nothing linked to your identity for us to export or delete on request. To erase everything Frameling stores locally (including your animations and the local anonymous analytics identifier), delete the app. For anything related to a purchase or this policy, contact Apple Support or write to us at xiangmucheng001@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
